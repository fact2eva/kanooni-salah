## 🔑 Changing Your ID and Password in the Future

You have complete control to change your login credentials anytime:

1. **While Logged in to the Causelist Diary**:
   - In the top action bar, click **"🔑 Change Password / ID"**.
   - Enter your current password to verify your identity.
   - Enter your new Advocate ID/Email (optional) and new password.
   - Click **Save New Credentials**. The credentials update immediately in your secure local vault.

2. **Recovering a Forgotten Password**:
   - On the login screen, click **"Forgot Password?"**.
   - Enter your registered Advocate ID or Email.
   - Click **Verify & Recover Password** for instant self-recovery without waiting for external emails or hitting SMTP quotas.

3. **Registering New Accounts (Sign Up)**:
   - On the login screen, click **"Sign Up (100% Free)"**.
   - Enter Full Name, Advocate ID/Email, and password to create unlimited counsel accounts.
